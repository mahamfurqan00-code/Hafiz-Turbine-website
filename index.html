ABOUT THE WORKSHOP

Trusted work since 1992

Hafiz Turbine and Engineering Works began as a small bench in Garhi Shahu and grew by word of mouth. We deal in water turbines, flanges, valves, pipes and turbine spare parts — and everything still leaves the workshop the way it always has: checked, tested and fitted by the people who handle it.

  • Built and tested in our own workshop
  • On-site fitting for homes, farms and industry
  • Honest quotes before any work begins
  • Water turbines, flanges, valves, pipes and spare parts
